Web Vitals Playground

Core Web Vitals are part of Google's page experience signals — and, more importantly, they are what people feel when a page is slow, jumpy or unresponsive. Each demo below has a bad and a good version, with a live overlay measuring the metric.

Demos

  • LCP — Largest Contentful Paint (loading): bad · good
  • CLS — Cumulative Layout Shift (visual stability): bad · good
  • INP — Interaction to Next Paint (responsiveness): bad · good
